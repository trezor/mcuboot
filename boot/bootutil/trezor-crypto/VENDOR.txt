Vendored from the trezor-firmware monorepo: crypto/
=====================================================

WHY VENDORED, not symlinked: this repository must build and be CI-able on its own.
A symlink into the monorepo (which is what this directory used to be) inverts the
dependency -- the monorepo pins THIS repo in nordic/trezor/west.yml, so nothing here
may point back at it, or the two form a cycle and mcuboot's own CI cannot resolve the
tree.

Safe to vendor because these are CRYPTOGRAPHIC PRIMITIVES: any correct SHA-2 /
Ed25519 implementation interoperates, so this copy drifting from the monorepo's cannot
break interop between the STM and the nRF. That is NOT true of the PQ image format
(leaf rule, TLV numbers, message construction) -- which is why that is pinned by the
cross-validation vectors in the monorepo's tests/fw_merkle instead of by sharing code.
